Heater Kit

How to Use

Click on the drum pads or use your keyboard keys (Q, W, E, A, S, D, Z, X, C) to play sounds.

Q - Heater 1
W - Heater 2
E - Heater 3
A - Heater 4
S - Clap
D - Open HH
Z - Kick n' Hat
X - Kick
C - Closed HH
